# Stage 3b: large maps, and the time of one replanning (2026-11-02)

`python cbm_bench.py cbm_bench_3b.csv --stage 3b --scens 1,2,3 --time-limit 60 --mem-limit 8000`,
`python replan_scaling.py replan_scaling.csv`, figure `figures/make_bench_figures.py cbm_bench_3b.csv figures/bench_3b --solved-only`.

- Maps: warehouse-10-20-10-2-1, Berlin_1_256 (MovingAI); empty-128-128 and empty-256-256 are OUR maps (not in
  the MovingAI set), made by `make_empty_maps.py` into `maps/`: random scen 1-3 with 1000 agents, starts pairwise
  distinct, goals pairwise distinct, uniformly from the largest 4-connected component (the whole map), seed
  size*100+s; last column = Manhattan distance (MovingAI writes the octile length; not used by our solvers).
- N: warehouse, empty-128 - 64, 128; Berlin, empty-256 - 64, 128, 256. Modes k4 / g5, scen 1-3, variants bs,
  bs-late, flow, floww; 60 s, 8000 MB, sequential, nothing else running. Worst case estimated 4.3 h (< 8 h, so flow
  stayed on the 256x256 maps); took 2.9 h. 240 runs.
- Statuses: solved / timeout only (no memory, no killed). Makespan equal on all 40 instances solved by several
  variants; every solution valid.
- In 62 of the flow/floww runs (all on empty-128, Berlin, empty-256) not a single replanning finished within 60 s
  (network size 0 / 0 in the table): their "replanning time" is the time until the abort, a lower bound only.
