# Step 5.1: penalties in Bulk Search, variant 1 (order of the expansions) (2026-11-03)

`--low bspen`: BulkSearchTeam with the routes of the other teams of the CT node (at the root: the teams planned
before); OPEN ordered by (accumulated penalty, then the key of Bulk Search). Entering cell v at step t through a move
u -> v adds (a) the number of other robots in v at t and (b) the number of other robots moving v -> u at the same
step (the weights of floww). Simplification of variant 1: waits inside a bulk cost nothing, cancelled (reversed)
edges keep the penalty of the parent, duplicates in a bulk are cut by the height as before (no (height, penalty)
pairs - that would be variant 2). The penalty of the routes is not minimal; T is the same.

Checks:
- `test_flow_team.py bs_team.exe --makespan 6000 --seed 11 --lows bspen` (and `--lows bs,bspen,flow,floww --t-start lb`):
  0 mismatches; `compare_exe.py bs_single bs_team`: 1124 instances, T, expansions and routes equal (bs unchanged).
- quality on the 2.2 instances (`--weights 3000 --seed 12 --low bspen`, 1981 solved): penalty of the bspen routes =
  brute-force minimum in 1420 (71.7%); mean excess 0.75, max 16; among the 561 with excess mean 2.66.
- `compare_cbm.py --low bspen` seeds 2/3, 3000 instances: 0 mismatches, node limit 2+4 (bs: 3+7).

bspen (tie-break conflicts), floww only on 3a; 60 s, 8000 MB, sequential. Worst case with bspen-late: ~13 h > 8 h,
